Fertilizers Based on Disease

Bacterial Blight

Organic Control

  • Clean cultivation
  • Use disease free seed
  • Use resistant variety - Ajay , PR10
  • Burn crop debris from previous crops

Chemical Control

  • Seed treatment with Streptocyclin 0.015% + Ceresan 0.05%
  • Hot water treatment- soak the seeds for 12hrs and then dip them in 53°c water for 30min
  • Spray Bacreriomycin @25g per hectare
  • Spray Agrimycin 0.025%

Leaf Blast

Organic Control

  • Clean cultivation
  • Use resistant seed variety like - Ratnagiri 68 , Karjat 1
  • Burn all crop debris from previous season
  • Seed treatment with 3% brine solution
  • Destroy collateral hosts like - Panicum repens , Brachiaria mutica

Chemical Control

  • Seed treatment with Thirum @3gm per kg seed
  • Spray Copper oxychloride @3ml per 10litre of water
  • Apply Bordeaux mixture 0.5%

Brown Spot

Organic Control

  • Field sanitation
  • Use resistant varieties- Bala , Krishna , Sabarmati
  • Use Psuedomonas fluoresence @10gm per kg seed
  • Hot water treatment to seed at 52-53°c for 10-15min before sowing

Chemical Control

  • Seed treatment with Thirum @3gm per kg seed
  • Spray Crop with Mancozeb 0.3%
  • Blitox50 @ 0.25%

Tungro Virus

Organic Control

  • Grow resistance variety- IR50 , CO45
  • Field sanitation
  • Destroy weed hosts of virus
  • Burn crop debris
  • Use light trap to trap Leaf hopper

Chemical Control

  • Spray Phosphamidon 0.1% , Monocrotophos 0.2%
  • Use neem oil 0.3%
  • Use manage leaf hopper sray Carbofuron 3.5kg per hectare

Rust

Organic Control

  • Remove collateral host like grasse
  • Use of disease free seeds
  • Crop rotation with non host species for 3-4 years
  • Apply Potash and phosphatic fertilizer to provide tolerance to crop like DAP, MOP, SSP
  • Avoid excessive use of nitrogen fertilezer

Chemical Control

  • Spray the crop with Dithane Z-78 @2gm per litre of water
  • spray sulphur dust @15-20kg per hectare
  • Spray Mancozeb @0.25 %
  • Spray Bordeaux mixture @1%

Fusarium wilt

Organic Control

  • Deep summer ploughing
  • Use resistance variety (different for each crop)
  • Crop rotation of 3-4 yrs with millets, sorghum, groundnut etc..
  • Sowing of seeds at deep depth (6-8cm in soil)
  • Soil solarization with mulches

Chemical Control

  • Seed treatment with Thiram or Captan @3-4gm per kg seed
  • Seed treatment with fungicide like Trichoderma viridae @4gm per kg seed
  • Soil drenching with Cabendazim @ 1gm per litre of water
  • Spray fungicide like Vitavax or Bavistin
