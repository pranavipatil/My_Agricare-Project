Quick Steps to Manage Bacterial Blight


  1. Keep the Field Clean

    2. After harvest, clean the field properly. Don’t leave old plant parts. It helps stop the disease.


  2. Use Healthy Seeds

    3. Always use seeds that are free from disease.


  3. Plant Strong Varieties

    4. Use rice varieties like Ajay or PR10 which can resist Bacterial Blight.


  4. Burn Old Crop Waste

    5. Burn the dried leaves and stems from the old crop to kill germs.


  5. Treat Seeds Before Sowing

    6. Treat your seeds with Streptocyclin (0.015%) and Ceresan (0.05%) before planting.


  6. Hot Water Method

    7. Soak seeds in water for 12 hours, then dip them in 53°C water for 30 minutes to kill bacteria.


  7. Spray Medicines on the Crop

    8. Spray Bacreriomycin at 25 grams per hectare when disease signs appear. Spray Agrimycin 0.025% solution on infected plants for control.

Tips:

  • Always wear gloves and protective gear when handling chemicals.
  • Spray early in the morning or late afternoon for better effect.
  • Check your field regularly for any signs of disease.
