Quick Steps to Manage Brown Spot


  1. Keep the Field Clean

    2. After harvest, clean the field properly. Remove old leaves and weeds to stop the disease from spreading.


  2. Use Strong Varieties

    3. Plant rice types like Bala, Krishna, or Sabarmati that don’t easily get infected.


  3. Use Helpful Bacteria on Seeds

    4. Mix seeds with Pseudomonas fluorescens (10g per kg of seeds) before sowing, It helps protect the plants.


  4. Hot Water Seed Treatment

    5. Soak seeds in warm water at 52–53°C for 10–15 minutes before planting, This kills hidden disease germs.


  5. Treat Seeds with Medicine

    6. Mix seeds with Thirum at 3g per kg of seeds to stop early disease infection.


  6. Spray Medicines on Plants

    7. Spray Mancozeb (0.3%) if disease signs appear. Also spray Blitox 50 (0.25%) on infected parts for better control.

Tips:

  • Don’t use too much nitrogen fertilizer—it can make the disease worse.
  • Keep some space between plants for good airflow.
  • Spray early morning or evening, and repeat if needed.
