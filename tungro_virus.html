Quick Steps to Manage Tungro Virus


  1. Select Healthy Seeds

    2. Start with seeds from trusted sources. Avoid seeds from infected fields.


  2. Choose Resistant Varieties

    3. Use rice types like IR50 or CO45, which can fight the Tungro virus better


  3. Clean the Field Before Planting

    4. Remove leftover crop parts, weeds, and infected plants to reduce chances of infection.


  4. Burn Crop Debris

    5. Burn dried leaves, stems, and other crop waste to kill virus-carrying insects.


  5. Remove Nearby Weeds

    6. Uproot wild grasses and weeds around the field that may carry the virus or attract insects.


  6. Set Up Light Traps

    7. Place light traps in the field to catch leaf hoppers (the insect that spreads the virus).


  7. Spray Insecticides

    8. Phosphamidon @ 0.1%, or Monocrotophos @ 0.2% to control their spread.

  8. Apply Neem Oil

    9. Spray Neem oil @ 0.3% on plants to naturally reduce insect attacks.

  9. Apply Insecticide in Soil

    10. Use Carbofuran @ 3.5 kg per hectare to kill insects from the root zone.

Tips:

  • Spray in early morning or evening for best results.
  • Don’t plant near Tungro-affected fields.
  • Regularly check plants for signs of infection.
