Quick Steps to Manage Rust Disease


  1. Use Healthy Seeds

    2. Start with clean, disease-free seeds to avoid early infection.


  2. Remove Collateral Hosts

    3. Uproot and remove nearby wild grasses and weeds that can carry the rust fungus.


  3. Rotate Crops

    4. Avoid planting the same crop again and again. Do crop rotation with non-host crops (like pulses or vegetables) for 3 to 4 years to break the disease cycle.


  4. Apply the Right Fertilizers

    5. Use DAP, MOP, or SSP (phosphatic and potash fertilizers) to make the plant stronger and help it tolerate disease.


  5. Avoid Too Much Nitrogen

    6. Don’t use too much urea or nitrogen fertilizers, as they make the plant soft and more likely to get rust.


  6. Spray Dithane Z-78

    7. Mix 2g of Dithane Z-78 per litre of water and spray it on the plants when you see rust spots.


  7. Dust Sulphur Powder

    8. Spray Sulphur dust @ 15–20 kg per hectare to control the spread of the fungus.

  8. Spray Mancozeb

    9. Use Mancozeb @ 0.25% (2.5g per litre) to protect healthy leaves

  9. Use Bordeaux Mixture

    10. Prepare and spray Bordeaux mixture @ 1% on affected plants as a strong fungal control.

Tips:

  • Spray in the early morning or late evening.
  • Repeat spraying every 10–12 days if needed.
  • Monitor the field regularly, especially after rain or cloudy weather.
