Quick Steps to Manage Fusarium Wilt


  1. Do Deep Summer Ploughing

    2. Plough the field deeply during hot summer days. This helps kill the fungus in the soil.


  2. Use Strong Varieties

    3. Choose crop types that don’t easily get this disease (check which variety is best for your crop)


  3. Rotate Crops for 3–4 Years

    4. Don’t grow the same crop every year. Grow millets, sorghum, or groundnut for 3–4 years to stop the fungus.


  4. Sow Seeds Deep in Soil

    5. Put seeds 6 to 8 cm deep into the soil while sowing. This helps plants grow strong and healthy.


  5. Cover Soil with Plastic Before Planting

    6. Keep plastic sheets (mulch) on the soil for 3–4 weeks in sunlight. This heats the soil and kills germs.


  6. Treat Seeds Before Sowing

    7. Mix seeds with Thiram or Captan (3–4g per kg of seeds) or Use Trichoderma viride (4g per kg of seeds) – a natural protection method.


  7. Pour Fungicide Near Roots

    8. Mix Carbendazim (1g in 1 litre of water) and pour it near the plant roots to kill fungus in the soil.

  8. Spray Fungicide on Plants

    9. Spray Vitavax or Bavistin on the crop to stop fungus from spreading.

Tips:

  • Don’t let water stand in the field — fungus grows faster in wet soil.
  • Clean your tools before using them in another field.
  • Watch plants daily and repeat spray if disease shows again.
