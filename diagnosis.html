Understand Symptoms to Identify Crop Diseases

Early diagnosis of crop diseases is essential for maintaining healthy plants and improving crop yield. At AgriCare, we provide step-by-step information based on common symptoms you observe in your crop. This helps farmers take immediate preventive or curative measures before the disease spreads further.

Although our website does not use deep learning or AI for prediction, we guide you with visual cues and practical knowledge to help identify possible diseases. Use our reference pages for each disease to understand symptoms and recommended fertilizers or treatments.


How to Diagnose

  1. Observe leaf color, shape, and any unusual spots.
  2. Check for insects or pests around affected plants.
  3. Touch the leaves and stems for roughness or stickiness.
  4. Look for stunted or wilted growth.
  5. Check if nearby plants have the same problem.
  6. Match the visible symptoms with the diseases listed on our website to find the right solution.
