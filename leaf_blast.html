Quick Steps to Manage Leaf Blast


  1. Clean the Field

    2. Remove weeds and infected plant remains after harvest to stop the spread of disease.


  2. Use Resistant Varieties

    3. Choose resistant varieties like Karjat 3, Phule Radha, and Ratnagiri 24 to prevent infection.


  3. Burn Crop Debris

    4. Burn dried leaves and leftover stubble from previous crops to destroy any leftover spores.


  4. Treat Seeds Organically

    5. Use brine solution (3%) to soak seeds and remove infected ones before sowing.


  5. Remove Collateral Hosts

    6. Uproot wild grasses such as Echinochloa colona and E. crusgalli that can host the blast fungus.


  6. Treat Seeds with Fungicide

    7. Use Thiram @ 2.5g/kg of seed before planting to reduce seed-borne infection.


  7. Spray Preventive Fungicides

    8. Apply Carbendazim @ 1g/litre of water or Tricyclazole @ 0.6g/litre of water at the early signs of infection. Repeat spraying after 10 days if needed.

Tips:

  • Maintain proper spacing and avoid overwatering.
  • Ensure good drainage to reduce moisture buildup.
  • Spray early in the morning or late afternoon for better results.
